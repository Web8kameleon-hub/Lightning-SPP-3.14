Good to know

Can I scan or print from this website?

You can upload a document or image to the browser demo to run the real document, Scan and Process stages. It accepts PDF, JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF and GIF files up to 8 MB. PDF files are validated and rendered through the Lightning document core before processing. Physical scanning and printing require a connected application and platform-specific adapters.

What is a license?

A paid license belongs to the verified account that uses the purchase email. Open Your account to view the existing key and its validity. Browser registration and activation of installed software are separate steps. If you already paid, do not purchase again to recover your key.

Who can help me get started?

Start with the free document demo or the installation guide. For a paid license, verify your email and sign in to Your account. If a verification email does not arrive, use Account recovery or contact support before paying.
