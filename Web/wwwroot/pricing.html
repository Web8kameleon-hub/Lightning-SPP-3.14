From signup to your license

  1. Create your customer account and verify your email.
  2. Sign in and choose a plan. Stripe Checkout shows the actual price and taxes before you pay.
  3. Return to Your account to check payment confirmation and view your existing license key. Email notification is an additional delivery channel.
  4. Use that key in the installed application activation flow. Registering a browser does not install or activate a separate application.

If verification email does not arrive, request another link or contact support. If you already paid, do not pay again: your stored key remains available after account verification.
