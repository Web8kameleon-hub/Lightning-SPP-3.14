PROBLEM:
IoTAPI.ProcessConstrained uses BitPipeline.
BitPipeline produces a CL-framed Brotli payload:
- Bytes 0-1: CL
- Byte 2: version 1
- Bytes 3-6: original length

BitUnpacker correctly decodes this format.

However, SdkCompressionTests attempts to decode the entire
CL-framed payload directly with BrotliStream.
Five tests fail with "Decoder ran into invalid data".
