We process personal data based on:

**Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR):**
- Providing software services
- User account management
- Technical support

**Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):**
- Improving our products
- Security and fraud prevention
- Network and information security

**Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR):**
- Compliance with medical device regulations
- Tax and accounting requirements
- Regulatory reporting

**Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):**
- Marketing communications
- Analytics (where required)
- Cookies (where applicable)

**Special Categories (Art. 9(2) GDPR):**
- Medical data processing under Art. 9(2)(h) - health or social care
- With explicit consent where required

### 5.2 Purposes of Processing

We use your data to:
- Provide and maintain our software
- Send administrative information
- Provide customer support
- Improve product quality
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce terms and conditions
- Protect against security threats

### 5.3 Medical Data Processing

For medical device deployments:

- **Legal Basis:** Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR + informed consent
- **Security:** Enhanced encryption and access controls
- **Retention:** As required by medical device regulations

## 8. Data Retention

### 8.1 General Data

- **Account Data:** Retained while account is active + 3 years
- **Usage Logs:** 12 months
- **Support Tickets:** 5 years
- **Contract Documents:** 10 years (legal requirement)

### 8.2 Medical Data

- **Patient Records:** As required by local regulations (typically 10-30 years)
- **Imaging Data:** Minimum 10 years (EU MDR requirement)
- **Audit Logs:** 15 years (medical device requirement)

### 8.3 Deletion

Data is deleted when:
- Retention period expires
- You request deletion (right to erasure)
- Account is closed (after retention period)
- Legal basis no longer applies

**Exception:** Data required by law is retained as mandated.

## 9. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

### 9.1 Right of Access (Art. 15)
Request a copy of your personal data

### 9.2 Right to Rectification (Art. 16)
Correct inaccurate or incomplete data

### 9.3 Right to Erasure (Art. 17)
Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")

### 9.4 Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18)
Limit how we use your data

### 9.5 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)
Receive your data in a machine-readable format

### 9.6 Right to Object (Art. 21)
Object to processing based on legitimate interests

### 9.7 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22)
Not subject to decisions based solely on automated processing

### 9.8 Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))
Withdraw consent at any time
